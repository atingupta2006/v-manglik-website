What We Offer

Our Professional Services

Professional support across taxation, GST, audit, compliance, representation and business-related registrations.

Direct Taxation GST Audit Company & LLP Startup & MSME Litigation Registrations
01

Direct Taxation

Support for individual and business taxation, TDS compliance, capital gains and related tax matters.

  • Income Tax Return (ITR) Filing
  • Litigation Support
  • Capital Gains Tax Advisory
  • NRI Taxation
  • TDS Compliance & Returns
  • Lower Deduction Certificates for TDS / TCS
02

Goods & Services Tax (GST) Services

GST registration, return filing, notices, litigation support and refund applications.

  • GST Registration
  • GST Return Filing
  • GST Notices & Litigation Support
  • GST Refund Application
03

Audit & Assurance

Professional audit services including statutory, tax and bank audits.

  • Statutory Audit
  • Tax Audit (Section 44AB)
  • Bank Audit
04

Company & LLP Services

Business formation and ROC compliance support for companies, LLPs and partnership firms.

  • Private Limited Company Registration
  • LLP Registration
  • OPC Registration
  • Partnership Firm Registration
  • ROC Annual Compliance
05

Startup & MSME Services

Registration support for startups and MSMEs under applicable schemes.

  • Startup India Registration
  • MSME/Udyam Registration
06

Litigation & Representation

Assistance with tax and GST appeals, assessments, notices and representation before authorities.

  • Income Tax Appeals
  • GST Appeals
  • Representation Before Tax Authorities
  • Assessment Proceedings
  • Reply to Notices
07

Other Registrations

Support for common registrations and related documentation requirements.

  • PAN & TAN
  • DSC (Digital Signature Certificate)
  • IEC Registration
  • LEI Registration
  • Trademark Coordination (through associates, if applicable)
